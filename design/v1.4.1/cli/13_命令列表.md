## 1. convert 命令组(新增)

```
convert <type> <value>            Convert typed data to hex bytes
                                  (type: byte|word|dword|qword|float|double|string|hex)
```

- 独立命令(无子动作),类型在前、数据在后;不需要 `-p`
- 输出单行空格分隔大写 hex 字节,可直接作为 `resolve scan` 的 pattern

### 使用示例

```
deeptrace_cli convert byte 255         ->  FF
deeptrace_cli convert word 0x0102      ->  02 01
deeptrace_cli convert dword 100        ->  64 00 00 00
deeptrace_cli convert qword 0x1122334455667788  ->  88 77 66 55 44 33 22 11
deeptrace_cli convert float 3.14       ->  C3 F5 48 40
deeptrace_cli convert double 1.0       ->  00 00 00 00 00 00 F0 3F
deeptrace_cli convert string hello     ->  68 65 6C 6C 6F
deeptrace_cli convert hex DEADBEEF     ->  DE AD BE EF

# 输出直接喂给扫描(两条独立命令):
deeptrace_cli convert dword 287454020              # 44 33 22 11
deeptrace_cli -p <pid> resolve scan "44 33 22 11"  # 命中 g_int
```
